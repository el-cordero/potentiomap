# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically
constructive community. Participants should be considerate, welcome
questions asked in good faith, focus criticism on ideas and evidence,
and respect privacy and data-use restrictions.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate
disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are
unacceptable. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is
harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the package maintainer at
<elvin.cordero@seamountgeo.com>. Reports will be reviewed as promptly
and confidentially as practical. This policy applies in project spaces
and when a person is publicly representing the project.

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
